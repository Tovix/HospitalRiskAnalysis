# Hospital Readmission Risk Analysis: Executive Business Summary

**For:** Hospital Administrators & Healthcare Executives  
**Date:** January 2025  
**Analysis Scope:** 100,000+ diabetic patient admissions (1999-2008)  

---

## 🎯 **Key Business Impact**

**Current Challenge:** Hospital readmissions cost the healthcare system billions annually and impact patient outcomes, quality ratings, and reimbursement rates.

**Opportunity:** Our data-driven analysis identifies specific, actionable interventions that can reduce readmissions by targeting the highest-risk patient populations and clinical factors.

---

## **Critical Findings**

### **1. Comorbidities Drive 3x Higher Readmission Risk**
- **Impact:** Patients with multiple conditions have **300% higher odds** of readmission (OR = 3.00)
- **Action Required:** Immediate implementation of integrated care coordination protocols
- **Target Population:** Focus on heart failure (ICD-9: 428) and coronary disease (ICD-9: 414) patients

### **2. Age is the Strongest Demographic Predictor**
- **Impact:** Readmission risk increases significantly after age 60
- **Action Required:** Age-stratified discharge planning and enhanced geriatric care protocols
- **Resource Allocation:** Prioritize additional support for patients over 60

### **3. Medication Management Affects Readmission Risk**
- **Protective Medications:** Metformin, Glipizide, Pioglitazone, Rosiglitazone show lower readmission rates
- **Risk Factor:** Medication changes during admission increase readmission risk by 10% (RR = 1.10)
- **Action Required:** Standardize medication selection protocols and enhance monitoring for medication changes

### **4. Gender and Racial Disparities Exist**
- **Impact:** Females and certain racial groups show higher readmission rates
- **Action Required:** Implement targeted interventions addressing healthcare equity

---

## **Immediate Action Plan (90-Day Implementation)**

### **Phase 1: High-Impact Interventions (30 days)**
1. **Deploy Comorbidity Risk Screening**
   - Implement automated flagging for patients with heart failure + diabetes
   - Establish dedicated care coordination teams for high-risk patients
   - **Expected Impact:** 15-20% reduction in readmissions for targeted population

2. **Age-Based Care Protocols**
   - Create enhanced discharge checklists for patients over 60
   - Implement mandatory geriatric consultation for high-risk elderly patients
   - **Expected Impact:** 10-15% reduction in elderly patient readmissions

### **Phase 2: Medication Optimization (60 days)**
1. **Standardize Diabetes Medication Selection**
   - Develop clinical decision support favoring protective medications when appropriate
   - Create medication change monitoring protocols
   - **Expected Impact:** 5-10% overall readmission reduction

2. **Enhanced Transition of Care**
   - Implement 48-72 hour post-discharge follow-up for medication changes
   - Establish pharmacist-led medication reconciliation programs

### **Phase 3: Population Health Initiatives (90 days)**
1. **Address Healthcare Disparities**
   - Develop gender-specific discharge planning protocols
   - Implement culturally competent care delivery models
   - **Expected Impact:** Improved equity metrics and patient satisfaction
